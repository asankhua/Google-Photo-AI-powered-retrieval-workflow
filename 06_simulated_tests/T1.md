## Post-task interview

**Moderator:** Which was easier for you, the standard search or Moment Finder? Why?

**T1:** Moment Finder, hands‑down. I typed one phrase and it popped up instantly—no endless scrolling or extra taps. The standard search made me hunt around, change terms and tap three times just to get nowhere.

**Moderator:** Would you use Moment Finder for your own child's photos? 1 to 5, and why.

**T1:** I’d give it a 5. It saved me minutes – just type what I’m looking for and the right pic shows up, instead of digging through years of photos. That’s exactly what I need with a toddler’s endless stream of moments.

**Moderator:** How sure were you that you'd picked the right photo, in each? 1 to 5.

**T1:** **First‑steps (standard search)** – 2 / 5  
I wasn’t confident; the results didn’t match the details I typed and I kept wondering if the right picture was hidden somewhere else.

**First‑steps (Moment Finder)** – 5 / 5  
The preview looked exactly like the one I wanted, so I was sure I’d got the correct photo on the first try.

**Cake‑smash (standard search)** – 1 / 5  
After eight taps and multiple searches I still wasn’t sure I’d found the right image – I kept scrolling back and forth, doubting each result.

**Cake‑smash (Moment Finder)** – 5 / 5  
The single result matched my query perfectly, so I was completely certain it was the right photo.

**Moderator:** Was anything uncomfortable, like the people or home chips, or the app knowing your family's faces?

**T1:** Honestly, the “people” and “home” chips felt a bit weird—like the app is watching who’s in my house. I’m okay with it if it just helps me find pics, but it makes me pause thinking about privacy.

**Moderator:** Is this how you'd search for your own child? What would you have tried next?

**T1:** No, I wouldn’t waste time typing long sentences into the generic search like I did with the standard view. I’d just open Moment Finder, type a short phrase (“first steps” or “cake smash”) and pick the result. If it didn’t show up right away, I’d try a slightly different keyword—maybe add the year or the location (“2023 first steps” or “home birthday”)—and let the chip suggestions narrow it down. That’s all I’d do before moving on to the next thing.

**Moderator:** Have you ever looked for the same photo more than once? Would 'save as milestone' help?

**T1:** Yeah, I’ve gone back for the same birthday or first‑step shot more than once – parents love re‑using them for cards, social posts, you name it. A “save as milestone” button would be a lifesaver; I could tag that picture once and have it pop up automatically whenever I need a birthday or growth‑milestone pic again. It’d cut out the whole re‑search hassle.

**Moderator:** Anything that confused you, or that you'd change?

**T1:** The chip labels threw me off – I wasn’t sure if “home” meant the house or a specific album, and the people chips kept popping up even when I just wanted a date‑based search. I’d also like a clear “back” button; I kept tapping “search” and then had to swipe back manually. Simplify the chip names (e.g., “Location: Home”) and give a quick way to clear or edit the search term, and it would feel a lot less confusing.
