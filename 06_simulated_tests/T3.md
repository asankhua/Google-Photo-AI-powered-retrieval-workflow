## Post-task interview

**Moderator:** Which was easier for you, the standard search or Moment Finder? Why?

**T3:** Moment Finder was definitely easier – I just typed “first steps” and it popped up right away. With the standard search I had to try a bunch of different combos, scroll through lots of results and still couldn’t find the right photo. The extra taps and guessing felt like a waste of time.

**Moderator:** Would you use Moment Finder for your own child's photos? 1 to 5, and why.

**T3:** I’d give it a 4. It saved me the hassle of fiddling with weird keyword combos and got the right picture instantly, which is huge when I’m juggling a toddler and a deadline. The only thing holding me back is still wondering how securely it handles all those family faces—if the privacy side is solid, I’d use it all the time.

**Moderator:** How sure were you that you'd picked the right photo, in each? 1 to 5.

**T3:** - Standard search (the “first day at daycare” and “first steps” attempts): **2** – I kept guessing keywords and scrolling, and even after the “first day at daycare” result I wasn’t 100 % sure it was the exact moment I wanted.  

- Moment Finder (both “first day at daycare” and “first steps”): **5** – It showed the exact photo I was looking for on the first try, so I was completely confident I had the right one.

**Moderator:** Was anything uncomfortable, like the people or home chips, or the app knowing your family's faces?

**T3:** Honestly, the idea of the app pulling out “people” or “home” chips makes me a bit uneasy – I don’t like the thought of it constantly recognizing my son’s face or our house and storing that info. If it’s just for my own device and encrypted, I could get used to it, but any hint that it’s sharing those tags elsewhere would definitely give me pause.

**Moderator:** Is this how you'd search for your own child? What would you have tried next?

**T3:** I’d probably stick with Moment Finder for quick stuff – type a simple phrase and hope it pulls the right shot. If it missed, I’d fall back to the old‑school Google Photos search and add extra tags (like “rain” or the date) or just scroll through the album manually. If the privacy chips kept popping up, I’d turn them off in settings before I trusted it with any more pictures.

**Moderator:** Have you ever looked for the same photo more than once? Would 'save as milestone' help?

**T3:** Yeah, I’ve gone back for the same clip‑on‑the‑floor video a few times – once to show my mom, once to make a birthday slideshow. Having a “save as milestone” button would be handy; I could tag that first‑step video once and then pull it up instantly without hunting through the whole library again. It’d save me the extra typing and scrolling every time I need that special moment.

**Moderator:** Anything that confused you, or that you'd change?

**T3:** The “people” and “home” chips that pop up automatically threw me off – I wasn’t sure if they were just suggestions or if the app was actually storing facial data somewhere. I’d like a clear toggle to turn those chips off or hide them completely. Also, the search bar in Moment Finder didn’t show any hint that it was using AI‑powered tags, so I wasn’t sure what keywords would work; a tiny tooltip like “search by event, person or location” would stop me from over‑thinking the phrasing. Other than that, the instant results were spot‑on, so those are the only things I’d tweak.
