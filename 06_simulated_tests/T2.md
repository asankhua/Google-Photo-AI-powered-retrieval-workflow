## Post-task interview

**Moderator:** Which was easier for you, the standard search or Moment Finder? Why?

**T2:** Standard search was easier – I just typed “first haircut” and got it in two taps. Moment Finder made me hunt around, filter and even failed to pull the haircut at all, so I ended up typing a long query and still wasn’t sure. It felt like extra work.

**Moderator:** Would you use Moment Finder for your own child's photos? 1 to 5, and why.

**T2:** I’d give it a 2. It sounds cool, but I spent more time fiddling with filters and long searches than just typing a simple keyword. For my kid’s photos I need something quick, not another layer of guessing.

**Moderator:** How sure were you that you'd picked the right photo, in each? 1 to 5.

**T2:** - First haircut – Moment Finder: 1 (I couldn’t even locate it, kept second‑guessing).  
- First haircut – Standard: 5 (the simple keyword hit the right picture, I was sure).  
- First day at daycare – Moment Finder: 4 (it popped up right away, but I still checked the details).  
- First day at daycare – Standard: 5 (two taps and the image matched what I was looking for).

**Moderator:** Was anything uncomfortable, like the people or home chips, or the app knowing your family's faces?

**T2:** Honestly, seeing chips that label “mom”, “dad” or “home” felt a bit weird – like the app is watching who’s in the picture. I’m okay with tagging my own albums, but the automatic face‑recognition feels too nosy for me, especially if it’s sharing that info somewhere else.

**Moderator:** Is this how you'd search for your own child? What would you have tried next?

**T2:** No, I’d just type what I need – “first haircut” or “daycare rain” – and tap the result. If the app didn’t show it right away I’d probably scroll through the timeline or narrow the date range manually instead of fiddling with the Moment Finder filters. That’s quicker for a busy parent.

**Moderator:** Have you ever looked for the same photo more than once? Would 'save as milestone' help?

**T2:** Yeah, I’ve gone back for the same clip a few times – the haircut when we’re making a birthday slideshow, the rainy daycare day when I’m showing grandparents. A “save as milestone” button would be handy; I could tag it once and then just pull up “milestones” later instead of hunting through thousands of shots every time.

**Moderator:** Anything that confused you, or that you'd change?

**T2:** The filters in Moment Finder are confusing – they ask “who was there?” or “newborn age?” and I’m not sure which to pick, so I end up typing a long query anyway. I’d ditch the extra filter steps and just let me type a keyword, then maybe a simple “pin as milestone” toggle after I pick the photo. Also, the face‑chips feel invasive; either hide them or make them optional.
